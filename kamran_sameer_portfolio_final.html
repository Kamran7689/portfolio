IS6147 · Service Design, UCC

Drip Loop: Circular Fashion Service for a Fast-Fashion Brand

Group service design project applying the Double Diamond framework to help a fast-fashion brand enter the sustainability space. I led the research phase, persona development, ideation, and Figma prototype work. The final concept centres on a QR Digital Product Passport embedded in each garment, unlocking a take-back scheme (Drip Loop) and an AI styling tool (Style Studio).

Field observation at Penneys, Cork: mapped store layout, 6 staff role interactions, and identified the critical gap — the brand disappears the moment the bag is handed over
Competitor analysis across Depop, Vinted, ThredUp, and Patagonia Worn Wear: no platform offers styling guidance or item provenance — the real gap is in the experience layer, not supply
Built the Ciarán Walsh persona and empathy map: revealed that trust and ease are bigger purchase barriers than price, shaping the entire Drip Coins reward mechanic
Championed three core pillars: Digital Passport (QR-linked item history), Drip Loop (subscription resale model), and AI Styling (outfit builder for secondhand items)
Identified EU Digital Product Passport regulation as a compliance-to-competitive-advantage pivot for the pitch
Produced lo-fi wireframes (buy, sell, restyle flows) and 5 hi-fi Figma screens: home, profile with sustainability score, browse, sell flow, and style tab

IS6144 · AI for Business, UCC

AI in Practice: Five Live Builds

Most AI assignments ask you to write about AI. This one asked you to build with it. Five working artifacts, each developed through iterative prompting, reflection, and improvement. A learning journal documents what changed and why.

Student Wellness Tracker: started as a basic tracker, iterated through goal-setting, streaks, dark mode, gamification (points, levels, achievements), and data insights across multiple build cycles
Netflix Data Dashboard: sourced a real Kaggle dataset, chose gap analysis as the framing, and iterated between aesthetics and actual meaning. Learning that design without insight is just decoration.
Heal Math: built a playable browser game from three disconnected creative constraints, going from an unplayable first version to a clean quiz mechanic where correct answers heal a character called Math
Creative Expansion Mode: designed a custom AI tool that takes a small idea and stretches it across multiple creative directions rather than returning one answer
Fashion Recommendation System: built in Python and Flask. Users input style preferences, size, and budget; backend filters and returns personalised outfit matches. Debugged and improved step by step until the matching logic worked accurately
